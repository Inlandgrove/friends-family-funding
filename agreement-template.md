## AGREEMENT

1. **Investment:** Investor agrees to provide the Investment Amount listed above to Inland Grove Golf Co. ("Company") via wire transfer to the Company's Mercury account.

2. **Revenue Share:** Beginning June 1, 2027, the Company will pay Investor the Revenue Share Percentage of the Company's gross monthly revenue until the Total Return amount has been paid in full.

3. **Payment Schedule:** Payments will be made monthly, within 15 days of each month's end, for revenue generated in the prior month.

4. **Cap:** Once the Investor has received payments totaling the Total Return amount, this agreement terminates and no further payments are due.

5. **No Equity:** This agreement does not grant the Investor any ownership, equity, or voting rights in the Company.

6. **Revenue Definition:** "Gross monthly revenue" means all revenue received by Inland Grove Golf Co. from all sources (wholesale, direct-to-consumer, licensing, etc.) before expenses.

7. **Reporting:** The Company will provide the Investor with monthly revenue summaries alongside each payment.

8. **Early Payoff:** The Company may, at its discretion, pay off the remaining balance early without penalty.

9. **Exit Event:** In the event of a sale, merger, or liquidation of the Company before the Investor has received their Total Return, the Company will pay the remaining balance owed to the Investor from the proceeds of such event.
